## Implemented behavior

- Source-bound transactional RoomOwner with tentative/confirmed/disputed states,
  stable identities, retained accepted geometry and bounded event/predecessor history.
- One-to-many DuDe proposals can remain associated with one Room while the proposed
  split is evaluated. Many-to-one proposals preserve multiple Room identities;
  their raw-region annotation is ambiguous rather than arbitrarily assigned.
- Split/merge requires repeated distinct measured evidence at shared internal edges
  and substantial current structural support. Opening a narrow doorway does not
  establish merge. A door object is optional corroboration, never a passage proof.
- Independent, axis-aligned 0.6–1.5 m occupancy gap candidates, with unique grid-bound
  IDs. They remain tentative and can be false positives or miss diagonal openings.
- UI overlays accepted Room and latest raw DuDe geometry separately on map and 3D;
  Room stability tab exposes state/reason/support/region associations/lineage.
  Existing raw-region object membership remains unchanged in this debug-first phase.
